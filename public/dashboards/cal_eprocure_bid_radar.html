calculate California 5% SB Bid Preference Calculator

Under California Government Code § 14838, certified Small Businesses receive a statutory 5% bid evaluation preference against the lowest responsive non-small business bid (capped at $50,000 for state IT solicitations).

Statutory note: The 5% preference is computed on the lowest non-small business price and subtracted for evaluation purposes. The winning certified Small Business is paid their full actual bid amount!
