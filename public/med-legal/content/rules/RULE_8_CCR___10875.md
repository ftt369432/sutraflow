# 8 CCR § 10875 (Formerly 8 CCR § 10770.1): Lien Conferences and Lien Trials

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
Lien Conference Appearance Mandate: Defendants must appear with full authority. Failure to appear triggers dismissal of objections and fee awards.

## ⚖️ Controlling Authority
8 CCR § 10875; LC § 4903.05
