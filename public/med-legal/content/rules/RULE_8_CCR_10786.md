# 8 CCR § 10786 (Formerly 8 CCR § 10451.1): Determination of Medical-Legal Expense Disputes & 60-Day Waiver

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
The 60-Day Default Waiver: Carrier waives all affirmative defenses (e.g., false history, post-term bar) if it fails to file a Petition for Determination within 60 days of provider's objection.

## ⚖️ Controlling Authority
Colamonico v. Secure Transportation (2019) 84 CCC 1059 (En Banc); LC § 4622
