# 8 CCR § 10635 (Formerly 8 CCR §§ 10608 / 10616): Ongoing Duty to Serve Medical Reports

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
The 10-Day Service Duty: Parties must serve medical reports within 10 calendar days of receipt. Defense cannot withhold prior accident records and then claim PTP/QME report lacks substantial evidence.

## ⚖️ Controlling Authority
8 CCR § 10635; Hardesty v. McCord (1976) 41 CCC 111 (En Banc)
