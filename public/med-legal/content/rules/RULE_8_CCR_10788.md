# 8 CCR § 10788 (Formerly 8 CCR § 10453): Petition for Automatic Reassignment of Judge

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
Peremptory WCJ Strike: Must be filed within 5 days of notice of hearing before a regular judge. Automatic assignment upon timely filing.

## ⚖️ Controlling Authority
8 CCR § 10788; LC § 5310
