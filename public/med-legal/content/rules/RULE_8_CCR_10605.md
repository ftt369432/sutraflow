# 8 CCR § 10605 (Formerly 8 CCR § 10507): Service Extension of Time (+5 Calendar Days)

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
Service by Mail/Fax/Email: Adds 5 calendar days for actions required within California. Critical for calculating EOR response deadlines and 25-day Reconsideration windows.

## ⚖️ Controlling Authority
8 CCR § 10605; LC § 5316; CCP § 1013
