# 8 CCR § 10752 (Formerly 8 CCR §§ 10563 / 10563.1): Appearances Required & Unlimited Settlement Authority

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
Settlement Authority Mandate: Defense adjusters and counsel must have immediate full settlement authority at MSC and hearings. Failure triggers mandatory sanctions under LC § 5813.

## ⚖️ Controlling Authority
8 CCR § 10752; LC § 5813; 8 CCR § 10421
