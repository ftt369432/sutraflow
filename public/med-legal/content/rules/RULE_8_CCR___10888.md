# 8 CCR § 10888 (Formerly 8 CCR § 10582.5): Dismissal of Inactive Lien Claims

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
Lien Preservation: Defense cannot obtain dismissal without formal Notice of Intention and verification of service.
