# 8 CCR § 10803(b) (Formerly 8 CCR §§ 10750 / 10751): Record of Proceedings Maintained in Adjudication File

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
The C&R Evidentiary Admission: All medical reports filed in EAMS as of the OACAR approval date are automatically deemed admitted into evidence as a matter of law.
