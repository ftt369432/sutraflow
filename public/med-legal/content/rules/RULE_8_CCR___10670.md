# 8 CCR § 10670 (Formerly 8 CCR § 10622): Documentary Evidence & Pre-Trial Exclusion (DPR Construction Strict Cutoff)

## 🔨 The Practice Hammer
Exclusion of Unlisted Evidence: Evidence not listed on the PTCS or served prior to MSC is excluded. Under DPR Construction (2025), Section 5502(d)(3) closure is jurisdictional and strictly NOT subject to harmless error review.

## ⚖️ Controlling Authority
DPR Construction v. WCAB (McClanahan) (2025) 107 Cal.App.5th ___; Rucker (2000); LC § 5502(d)(3)
