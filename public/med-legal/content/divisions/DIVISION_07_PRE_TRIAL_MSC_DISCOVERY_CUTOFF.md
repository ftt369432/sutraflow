## Chapter 69: Dismissal For Lack Of Prosecution

DOI Before 1/1/94

No Activity for 180 days Applies only to claims filed before 1/1/94
and where no application for adjudication has been filed.

LC§5404.5; R§10583, Alfredo Moran v.
Bradford Building, Inc. 57 CCC 273 (only
grounds for dismissal is LC§5404.5 — cannot
use CCP.)

If Claim Denied -- Serve Notice. Where there has been no activity
for 180 days the claims admin must have injured worker served
personally (see section in this book on serving uninsured employers)
with notice that the claim will be dismissed by operation of law
(automatically) unless an application for adjudication is filed within
180 days of service of the notice.

If Claim Accepted -- Serve Notice. Where there has been no
activity for 180 days, the claims admin must have the injured worker
personally served (the way a Summons is served) with the notice
that the claim will be dismissed by operation of law (automatically)
unless an application for adjudication is filed within 5 years of the
date of injury or within one year of the last furnishing of benefits,
whichever is later.

»If the worker receives either of the above notices they should
immediately file the application for adjudication if they wish to
| pursue their claim.

If the period to file the application for adjudication has expired the
claim is automatically dismissed.

DOI after 1/1/94

After an application for adjudication is filed, the case is on inactive
status until a Declaration of Readiness (DOR) is filed.

attached to the petition. The petition must be served on all
_parties and lien claimants.

(OTOC), the case becomes inactive.

If a case remains inactive for one year, the application may be R§10582
dismissed after notice and opportunity to be heard.

Defendant to file Petition to Dismiss for Lack of Prosecution. R§10582

72, REASSIGNMENT TO ANOTHER WCJ

Automatic Reassignment

An injured worker shall be entitled to one reassignment of a judge
for trial or expedited hearing. If the injured worker has not
exercised the right to automatic reassignment and one or more lien
claimants have become parties and no testimony has been taken, the
lien claimants shall be entitled to one reassignment of judge for trial,
which may be exercised by any of them.

R8 CCR § 10788 [formerly Rule 10453 - Petition for Automatic Reassignment of Judge]

The defendants shall be entitled to one reassignment of judge for a
trial or expedited hearing, which may be exercised by any of them.

R8 CCR § 10788 [formerly Rule 10453 - Petition for Automatic Reassignment of Judge]
