## Chapter 83: Petition For Removal

LC§5310

The board’s power to remove a case to itself under LC§5310 is an
extraordinary remedy, rarely exercised by board, and it is generally
available only where the petitioner has established that substantial or
irreparable injury will result if removal is not granted.

Final Order. If not a final order, can’t do petition for

## Chapter 84: Petition To Reopen

»Calendar deadline date.

Deadline (SOL) is 5 years from date of injury. Employer cannot file
a petition to terminate a precautionary medical treatment award after
five years. The Board retains, however, continuing jurisdiction
pursuant to section 5803 to adjudicate an employer’s claim that a
particular medical treatment is unjustified or not attributable to the
original injury.

LC§5410, 5804; Barnes v. WCAB (2000) 23
C4th 679, 65 CCC 780 (Supreme Court);
Fekkers v. WCAB 67 CCC 92 (wd) (not only
must petition be filed within 5 years, 5804,
but new and further disability must develop
within 5 years, 5410.); Beck v. WCAB 65 CCC
845

issued 2 years prior to filing of applicant’s petition to re-open;
jurisdiction is lost after statutory time for seeking reconsideration
has expired.
