Trial Procedures

Prior to start of trial WCJ will inquire if all parties are present and
ready to commence the trial and if there are any preliminary
motions.

The WC will then have the court reporter present and go on the
record — stating the name of the case and case numbers and that the
hearing is for trial. Multiple cases will be consolidated. The WCJ
will ask each party to state their appearance, i.e. his or her name and
who they represent. The WCJ will then state the issues, stipulations
and the exhibits and ask each party if the issues and list of exhibits
are correct. It is advisable for counsel to write down the issues and
list of exhibits as stated by the WCJ. This is the road map for the
case. The applicant must present some evidence on each issue
except for those issues that are affirmative defenses such as the
SOL. When the defense presents evidence on an issue the issue must
be proven by preponderance of evidence. Objections to the exhibits
may then be made. If there is no objection to an exhibit it is
admitted into evidence. Opening statements are not done.

‘A motion can be made by any party to exclude witnesses. Each
party is allowed to have one witness remain in the courtroom. The
WC] will then ask the applicant’s attorney to call the first witness.

The order of witnesses is not usually important. Applicant usually
calls other witnesses before the applicant testifies so that the other
witnesses can be excused and the applicant can get the benefit of
hearing the other witnesses’ testimony before he or she testifies.

The attorneys should take good notes of the testimony because
transcripts are not prepared.

When the applicant rests the defense may present witnesses. After
the defense rests each side is allowed to present rebuttal evidence.
All witnesses and exhibits must have been listed on the stips and
issues at the time of the mandatory settlement conference.

At the end of the trial the WCJ will either accept into evidence or
exclude exhibits for which there was an objection. The WC] will
then state that the matter stands submitted. The trial has then
concluded.

R§10600, 10630
