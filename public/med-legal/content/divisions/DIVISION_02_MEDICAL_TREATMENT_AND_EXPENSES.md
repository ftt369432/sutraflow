## Chapter 17: Hco Treatment

This section applies where the injured employee is enrolled in
an HCO.

If the employer has entered into a contract with HCOs and the
employee has not designated a personal physician the
employee must treat with the HCO designated by the employer]
for 90 or 180 days.

LC§4600.3, R§9779.3; Chen v. WCAB 68 CCC 935

If the employee has designated a personal physician before the
injury the employee may be treated by the physician. No
waiting period.

If the pre-designated personal physician declines to treat,
employee can choose another PTP.

LC§4600.3; Withers v. The May Dept. Stores (2001) 3
CWCR 15

When the employee is not receiving or is not eligible to
receive health care coverage paid at least by 50% by the
employer for non-occupation injuries the employee may
choose a new PTP after 90 days. The choice must be in
writing.

LC§4600.3(c)(1)

When the employee is receiving or is eligible to receive health
care coverage paid at least 50% by the employer for non-
occupational injuries the employee may choose a new PTP
after 180 days from the date the injury was reported

LC§4600.3(c)(2)

An employee being treated by an HCO shall have the right to
one change of physician within the HCO. The change must be
provided within 5 days of the receipt of the request. The
employee shall also have the right to a second opinion from
another physician in the HCO.
