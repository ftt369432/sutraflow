Body Parts

Defendant would like to include as many body parts as possible.
Language such as “all body parts mentioned in the medical reports
of Dr. Jones” is generally not agreed upon by applicant’s attorneys.
Applicant’s attorneys should beware of body parts listed for prior
injuries or awards or for nonindustrial injuries that are covered by
other insurance or Medicare. Also, applicants want to limit the
listing of body parts because of the possibility of apportionment in
future cases.
