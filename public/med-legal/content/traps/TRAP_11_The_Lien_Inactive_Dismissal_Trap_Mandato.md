# ⚡ Defense Trap #11: The Lien Inactive Dismissal Trap (Mandatory Notice of Intention)

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** 8 CCR § 10888, Labor Code § 4903.5
- **Controlling Precedent:** 8 CCR § 10888 Regulatory Precedent

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Carrier moves to dismiss lien for lack of prosecution without serving formal Notice of Intention or without proving proper service of trial notices.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
Dismissal order is void for violation of due process. Lien is reinstated with statutory interest running under LC § 5800.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. File Petition for Reconsideration within 25 days under 8 CCR § 10888.
2. Establish lack of statutory 10-day Notice of Intention.
3. Demand reinstatement and setting for Lien Conference.
