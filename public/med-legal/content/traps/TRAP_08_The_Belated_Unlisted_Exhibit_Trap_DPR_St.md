# ⚡ Defense Trap #8: The Belated / Unlisted Exhibit Trap (DPR Strict Bar)

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** LC § 5502(d)(3), 8 CCR § 10670
- **Controlling Precedent:** DPR Construction v. WCAB (McClanahan) (2025)

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Carrier dumps unlisted medical records, MPN letters, or investigation reports on FileNet after the MSC or on the eve of trial.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
Immediate mandatory exclusion of evidence. Annulment of any award relying on unlisted evidence. Harmless error and lack of prejudice defenses are legally barred.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. Object on the record at trial.
2. Cite DPR Construction (2025) holding that Section 5502(d)(3) is jurisdictional and NOT subject to harmless error.
3. Demand order excluding exhibit and striking defense reliance.
