# ⚡ Defense Trap #10: The Scattershot & Boilerplate Defense Objection Trap

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** Labor Code § 5813, 8 CCR § 10421
- **Controlling Precedent:** Super Wash Express v. WCAB (Garcia) (2009); Martin (1997)

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Carrier files shotgun, scattershot objections to DOR, medical treatment, or DOR trial without verifying factual basis or conducting reasonable investigation.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
Sanctions up to $2,500 payable to State General Fund plus mandatory applicant attorney's fees under LC § 5813 and 8 CCR § 10421.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. Issue 10-day safe harbor meet-and-confer demanding defense withdraw scattershot objections or provide evidence.
2. Subpoena claims adjuster investigation notes.
3. File Petition for Sanctions and Attorney's Fees under LC § 5813 citing Super Wash Express.
