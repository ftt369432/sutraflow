# ⚡ Defense Trap #6: Unreasonable Delay Penalty Multiplier

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** Labor Code § 5814, § 5814.5
- **Controlling Precedent:** Kerley (1971) / Barnes (2005) / Ramirez

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Carrier delays TTD, PD, or medical mileage without genuine medical or legal doubt.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
25% penalty up to $10,000 per separate act of delay + mandatory attorney's fees under LC § 5814.5.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. Send formal demand with 10-day cure.
2. Calculate exact 25% penalty on all delayed sums.
3. File LC § 5814 petition + § 5814.5 fee petition.
