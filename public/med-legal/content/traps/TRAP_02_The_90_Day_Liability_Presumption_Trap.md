# ⚡ Defense Trap #2: The 90-Day Liability Presumption Trap

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** Labor Code § 5402(b)
- **Controlling Precedent:** Welcher (1995) 31 Cal.App.4th 90

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Carrier delays decision on DWC-1 claim form past 90 days without issuing written Notice of Denial.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
Injury is presumed compensable AOE/COE. All evidence discoverable within the 90 days is EXCLUDED at trial.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. File Motion in Limine on Day of Trial.
2. Exclude defense QMEs, sub-rosa video, and witness declarations obtained after 90 days.
