# ⚡ Defense Trap #9: The MSC Discovery Cutoff Trap / DPR Construction Hammer

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** Labor Code § 5502(d)(3), 8 CCR § 10670
- **Controlling Precedent:** DPR Construction et al. v. WCAB (McClanahan) (2025)

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Carrier attempts to introduce unlisted medical reports, surveillance, or personnel records at trial, arguing absence of prejudice or harmless error.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
Evidence is strictly inadmissible. Section 5502(d)(3) discovery closure is jurisdictional and strictly NOT SUBJECT TO HARMLESS ERROR ANALYSIS. Lack of prejudice cannot excuse carrier failure to list evidence on PTCS.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. Object immediately on the record at trial call under LC § 5502(d)(3).
2. Cite DPR Construction (2025) and McKenzie (1999).
3. State: 'Admitting unlisted exhibits exceeds Board jurisdiction and cannot be excused by harmless error or lack of prejudice.'
4. Move to exclude exhibit with prejudice.
