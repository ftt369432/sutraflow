# ⚡ Defense Trap #7: Duty to Pay upon QME Finding

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** Labor Code § 4063
- **Controlling Precedent:** LC § 4063 Statutory Mandate

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
State Panel QME issues report finding industrial injury/disability; carrier maintains blanket denial.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
Direct statutory violation; eliminates genuine legal doubt; triggers maximum § 5814 penalties and sanctions.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. Serve QME report with formal demand under LC § 4063.
2. If unpaid within 14 days, file § 5814 penalty petition.
