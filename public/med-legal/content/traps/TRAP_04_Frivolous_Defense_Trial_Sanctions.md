# ⚡ Defense Trap #4: Frivolous Defense Trial Sanctions

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** LC § 5813, 8 CCR § 10421
- **Controlling Precedent:** Tito Torres (2012) 77 CCC 1113 (En Banc)

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Defense counsel pushes matter to trial without substantial medical evidence, asserting baseless or contradicted defenses.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
Monetary sanctions + full reasonable attorney's fees + costs awarded against carrier and defense attorney jointly/severally.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. Serve 10-day Meet & Confer letter under 8 CCR § 10761.
2. File Petition for Sanctions under LC § 5813.
3. Attach Exhibit 1 itemized time declaration.
