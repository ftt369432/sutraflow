# ⚡ Defense Trap #3: The 45-Day COVID Outbreak Default

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** Labor Code § 3212.88
- **Controlling Precedent:** Statutory COVID Outbreak Rule

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Carrier fails to reject liability within 45 days for employee who tested positive during active workplace outbreak.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
Industrial causation is presumed by law. Carrier barred from introducing prior discoverable evidence.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. Establish outbreak via employer COVID tracker/PMQ depo.
2. Prove positive PCR within 14 days of work.
3. Move for compensability finding.
