# ⚡ Defense Trap #1: The Defective UR / Dubon Invalidation

- **Governing Law / Regulation:** LC § 4610, 8 CCR § 9792.9.1
- **Controlling Precedent:** Dubon II / Bodam / Castillo (En Banc)

## 🚫 Common Defense Blunder
Carrier/UR issues denial late (over 5 business days from complete RFA) or fails to telephone/fax requesting doctor within 24 hours of decision.

## 🎯 Affirmative Remedy & Sanctions
UR decision is legally void; IMR does NOT apply; WCAB Judge has full jurisdiction to award requested medical treatment based on PTP report.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Tactical Roadmap
1. Subpoena UR fax logs & RFA receipt timestamp.
2. File DOR on Expedited Medical Treatment.
3. Request finding of invalid UR under Dubon II.
