# Navarro v. City of Montebello (2014) 79 Cal.Comp.Cases 418

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2014)
- **Category:** Additional QME Specialty Panels
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4062.2, 8 CCR § 37.1

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
A party is entitled to an additional QME panel in a different medical specialty when the existing QME recommends evaluation in another specialty or when a distinct condition falls outside the existing QME's expertise.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The Medical Unit may issue an additional panel QME in a different specialty when substantial medical evidence establishes that the injured worker has sustained an injury involving a distinct medical specialty."
