# Gillette v. Workmen's Comp. Appeals Bd. (1971) 20 Cal.App.3d 312, 36 CCC 570

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (3rd Dist.) (1971)
- **Category:** AOE / COE & Cumulative Trauma
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3208.1, § 3600

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Cumulative trauma / repetitive micro-trauma occurring gradually over time is fully compensable even without a specific traumatic event.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An injury may be cumulative and occur gradually over a period of time where repetitive physical work activities aggregate to produce pathology."
