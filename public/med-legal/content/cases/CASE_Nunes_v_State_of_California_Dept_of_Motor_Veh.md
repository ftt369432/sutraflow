# Nunes v. State of California / Dept. of Motor Vehicles (2023) 88 Cal.Comp.Cases 741 & 88 CCC 894

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2023)
- **Category:** Multiple Injuries Apportionment
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4663, § 4664

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Reporting physicians must apportion permanent disability between separate, distinct industrial injuries before applying the Combined Values Chart (CVC), unless reporting proves inexorably intertwined disability.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "A physician must evaluate and determine apportionment to other injuries before combining multiple impairments under the rating schedule."
