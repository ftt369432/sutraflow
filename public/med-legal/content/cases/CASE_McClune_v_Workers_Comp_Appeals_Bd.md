# McClune v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1998) 62 Cal.App.4th 1117, 63 CCC 261

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (4th Dist.) (1998)
- **Category:** Due Process in Record Development
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5701, U.S. Const. 14th Amend.

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Due process requires that when medical evidence is ambiguous or incomplete, the WCJ must develop the record rather than cutting off the injured worker's right to prove compensability.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Due process requires that the parties be provided a full and fair opportunity to present evidence, and the Board has an obligation to order development of the record to determine industrial causation."
