# McCoy v. Industrial Acc. Comm'n (1966) 64 Cal.2d 82, 31 CCC 93

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1966)
- **Category:** Self-Procured Medical Care Reimbursement
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4600, § 4603.2

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
When an employer or carrier fails, neglects, or refuses seasonably to provide reasonable medical care, the employee may self-procure treatment outside the network at the employer's expense.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "If the employer has notice of the injury and fails or refuses to provide treatment, the employee may procure it himself and the employer is liable for the reasonable expense incurred."
