# Tyler v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1997) 56 Cal.App.4th 389, 62 CCC 924

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (2nd Dist.) (1997)
- **Category:** Judge's Authority to Order Medical Exam
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5701, § 5906

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
The WCJ possesses broad inherent statutory authority under LC §§ 5701 and 5906 to order the taking of further medical evidence or appoint an independent medical evaluator whenever necessary to determine the truth.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The workers' compensation judge has the authority to order the taking of further medical evidence or appoint an independent medical evaluator where existing reporting is unsatisfactory."
