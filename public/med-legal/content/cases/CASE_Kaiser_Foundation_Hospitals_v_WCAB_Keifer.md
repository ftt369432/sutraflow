# Kaiser Foundation Hospitals v. WCAB (Keifer) (1974) 13 Cal.3d 20, 39 CCC 857

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1974)
- **Category:** No Unauthorized Reduction of Valid Liens
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4903, § 4906

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
The WCAB has no authority to reduce the amount of a valid medical lien or award a compromised fraction of the lien without an evidentiary hearing and statutory basis.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The Board has no statutory authority to reduce the amount of a valid lien for medical services without a full evidentiary hearing and legal justification."
