# Super Wash Express v. WCAB (Garcia) (2009) 74 Cal.Comp.Cases 1459

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board / Panel Precedent (2009)
- **Category:** Bad-Faith Litigation & Scattershot Objections Sanctions
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5813, 8 CCR § 10421

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Filing blanket, scattershot, or boilerplate objections and petitions without an evidentiary foundation or without conducting a reasonable investigation is frivolous and in bad faith under Labor Code § 5813, justifying monetary sanctions and applicant attorney's fees.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Asserting scattershot and blanket defense objections without factual verification or evidence constitutes bad-faith, frivolous tactics warranting sanctions under section 5813."
