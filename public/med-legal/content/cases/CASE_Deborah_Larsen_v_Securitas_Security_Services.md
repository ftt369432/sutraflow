# Deborah Larsen v. Securitas Security Services (2016) 81 Cal.Comp.Cases 770

- **Court:** WCAB Panel Decision (2016)
- **Category:** Violent Act (No Criminal Actor Required)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4660.1(c)(2)(A)

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
A 'violent act' under LC § 4660.1(c)(2)(A) does not require criminal intent or a criminal perpetrator; extreme kinetic physical force or vehicular collision suffices.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "A 'violent act' under Labor Code section 4660.1(c)(2)(A) does not require criminal intent or a third-party perpetrator; it encompasses injuries caused by strong or violent physical force."
