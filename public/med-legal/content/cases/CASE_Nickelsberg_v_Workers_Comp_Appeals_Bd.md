# Nickelsberg v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1991) 54 Cal.3d 288, 56 CCC 476

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1991)
- **Category:** 5-Year TTD Cap & Reopening Jurisdiction
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4656, § 5410, § 5804

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
The WCAB lacks jurisdiction to award temporary total disability (TTD) indemnity more than five years after the date of injury unless a timely petition to reopen for new and further disability was filed within five years under LC § 5410.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Labor Code section 4656 establishes a strict limitation on temporary disability indemnity... the Board cannot award temporary disability beyond the five-year period absent a timely petition to reopen."
