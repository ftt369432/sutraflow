# Martin v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1997) 59 Cal.App.4th 333, 62 CCC 1500

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (2nd Dist.) (1997)
- **Category:** Discovery Abuse & Scattershot Privilege Assertions Barred
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5710, Evid. Code § 917

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Carriers cannot assert blanket or scattershot claims of attorney-client or work-product privilege to suppress discoverable claim logs and investigation notes. Defendants are mandated to provide an itemized privilege log.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Blanket, scattershot assertions of privilege are prohibited; a party asserting privilege must specify each individual document and the specific legal ground for withholding it."
