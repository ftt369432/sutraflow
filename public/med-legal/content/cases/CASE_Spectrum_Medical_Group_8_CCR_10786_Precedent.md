# Spectrum Medical Group / 8 CCR § 10786 Precedent 8 CCR § 10786(f)

- **Court:** DWC / WCAB Regulatory Rule (2023)
- **Category:** Medical-Legal Expenses & 60-Day Waiver
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4620, § 4622, 8 CCR § 10786(f)

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Under 8 CCR § 10786(f), if a defendant fails to file a Petition for Determination of Medical-Legal Expenses within 60 days of receiving a timely Objection to EOR, all non-threshold defense objections are waived as a matter of law.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "If the employer fails to file a petition within 60 days of service of the provider's objection, all objections to the billing other than threshold issues are deemed waived."
