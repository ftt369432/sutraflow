# Zenith Insurance Co. v. WCAB (Capi) (2006) 138 Cal.App.4th 373, 71 CCC 374

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (2nd Dist.) (2006)
- **Category:** Burden of Proof for Lien Claimants
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5705, Evid. Code § 500

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Lien claimants bear the burden of proving industrial injury and reasonable medical necessity by a preponderance of the evidence, 'standing in the shoes' of the injured worker.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Under Labor Code section 5705 and Evidence Code section 500, a lien claimant has the burden of proving all elements necessary to establish its claim for reimbursement."
