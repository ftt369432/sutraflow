# Mercer-Fraser Co. v. Industrial Acc. Com. (1953) 40 Cal.2d 102, 18 CCC 3

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1953)
- **Category:** Serious & Willful Misconduct (S&W)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4553

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Serious and Willful Misconduct under LC § 4553 requires proving that the employer intentionally committed an act with knowledge that serious injury was a probable result, or with reckless disregard.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Serious and willful misconduct is not mere negligence or gross negligence; it involves intentional conduct with knowledge that serious injury is likely to result."
