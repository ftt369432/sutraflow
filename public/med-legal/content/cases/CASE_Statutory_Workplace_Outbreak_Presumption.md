# Statutory Workplace Outbreak Presumption Cal. Lab. Code § 3212.88

- **Court:** California Statute / WCAB Precedent (2020)
- **Category:** AOE / COE Presumptions (COVID Outbreak)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3212.88 (SB 1159)

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Creates a rebuttable presumption of industrial causation for employees testing positive within 14 days of working during an active workplace outbreak. Defendant has only 45 days to reject liability.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "If liability is not rejected within 45 days after the claim is filed, the injury shall be presumed compensable. This presumption is rebuttable only by evidence discovered subsequent to the 45-day period."
