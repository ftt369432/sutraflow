# Brenda Gonzalez v. Insource Staffing ADJ20228986 (WCAB Decision on Recon)

- **Court:** WCAB Panel Decision (2026)
- **Category:** Medical Report Basis for C&R Adequacy
- **Controlling Authority:** 8 CCR § 10803(b), Labor Code § 5001

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Where a medical provider's report was relied upon to establish the adequacy of a Compromise & Release settlement, the defendant is equitably estopped from denying the industrial compensability of that provider's services.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "When a medical report serves as the foundation for the Court's approval of a Compromise and Release, the defendant cannot subsequently disclaim the industrial basis of that provider's treatment."
