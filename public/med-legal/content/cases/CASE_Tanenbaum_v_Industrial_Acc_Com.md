# Tanenbaum v. Industrial Acc. Com. (1935) 4 Cal.2d 615

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1935)
- **Category:** AOE / COE & Dormant Pathology (Lighting Up)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3600, § 4663

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Where employment activity lights up or activates a previously dormant, non-disabling pre-existing pathology, the resulting disability is industrial. Apportionment is only permitted if the pre-existing pathology was independently producing disability prior to industrial aggravation.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The acceleration, aggravation, or 'lighting up' of a pre-existing disease or condition by work constitutes an industrial injury."
