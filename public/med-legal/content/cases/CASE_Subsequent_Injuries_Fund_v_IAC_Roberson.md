# Subsequent Injuries Fund v. IAC (Roberson) (1963) 59 Cal.2d 842, 28 CCC 143

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1963)
- **Category:** Med-Legal Reimbursement Regardless of Success
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4620, § 4621

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
A workers' compensation claimant (and medical-legal provider) is entitled to reimbursement for medical-legal expenses reasonably incurred to prove a claim regardless of whether the claimant ultimately prevails.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An employee is entitled to reimbursement for medical-legal expenses reasonably and necessarily incurred to prove a contested claim whether he is successful or not."
