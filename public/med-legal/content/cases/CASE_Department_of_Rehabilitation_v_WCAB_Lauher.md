# Department of Rehabilitation v. WCAB (Lauher) (2003) 30 Cal.4th 1281, 68 CCC 831

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (2003)
- **Category:** 132a Prima Facie Case & Business Necessity
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 132a

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
To establish a prima facie case under LC § 132a, an employee must prove disadvantage caused by the industrial injury or claim. The employer may defend by proving 'business necessity.'

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An employee must show that he or she suffered some disadvantage or detriment caused by the employer's discriminatory act... the employer can avoid liability by proving real and reasonable business necessity."
