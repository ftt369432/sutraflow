# Brodie v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (2007) 40 Cal.4th 1313, 72 CCC 565

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (2007)
- **Category:** Apportionment 'Formula C' Calculation
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4663, § 4664

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Establishes 'Formula C' as the controlling method for calculating permanent disability indemnity dollar awards after non-industrial apportionment under SB 899.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Apportionment under SB 899 requires calculating the percentage of permanent disability directly caused by the industrial injury and awarding the dollar value corresponding to that percentage."
