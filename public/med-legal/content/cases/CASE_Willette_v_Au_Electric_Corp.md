# Willette v. Au Electric Corp. (2004) 69 Cal.Comp.Cases 1298

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2004)
- **Category:** Post-UR Dispute Resolution & QME Primacy
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4600, § 4062

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Established the mandatory procedure for resolving medical disputes when UR does not approve care; panel QME reporting must consider both PTP and UR reports; authority to award 24-hour attendant care.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "When faced with differing medical opinions from the Panel QME, treating physician, and UR physician... the WCJ or Appeals Board need not rely on the opinion of a particular physician but evaluates substantial evidence."
