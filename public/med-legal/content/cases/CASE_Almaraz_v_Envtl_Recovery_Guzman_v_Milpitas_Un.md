# Almaraz v. Envtl. Recovery / Guzman v. Milpitas Unified (2009) 74 Cal.Comp.Cases 1084 (Almaraz/Guzman II)

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2009)
- **Category:** AMA Guides Rebuttal (Almaraz/Guzman)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4660, 8 CCR § 9805

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
The AMA Guides 5th Edition rating is rebuttable. An evaluating physician may utilize any chapter, table, or analogy within the four corners of the Guides that most accurately reflects true impairment.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "A physician may offer an alternative rating based on the four corners of the AMA Guides if the standard rating does not accurately reflect the employee's whole person impairment."
