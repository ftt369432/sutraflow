# Purvis Cowens v. ABC Unified School District ADJ13906645 (WCAB Decision on Recon)

- **Court:** WCAB Panel Decision (2022)
- **Category:** Attorney Fees on Delayed Ancillary Benefits
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5814.5

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Attorney's fees under LC § 5814.5 apply not only to delayed indemnity but also to unreasonable delays in providing ancillary medical benefits and medical transportation.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An unreasonable delay in providing medical transportation or ancillary benefits triggers entitlement to statutory penalties under section 5814 and attorney's fees under section 5814.5."
