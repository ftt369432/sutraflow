# Kaiser Foundation Hospitals v. WCAB (Martin) (1985) 39 Cal.3d 57, 50 CCC 507

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1985)
- **Category:** Tolling of SOL for Failure to Give Notice
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5402, § 5405

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
An employer's failure to provide statutory notices of workers' compensation rights under LC § 5402 tolls the statute of limitations for the filing of medical provider claims/liens until notice is given.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The statute of limitations for filing a claim or lien is tolled where the employer fails to notify the employee of his workers' compensation rights upon receiving notice of injury."
