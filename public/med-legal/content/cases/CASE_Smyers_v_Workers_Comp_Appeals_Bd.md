# Smyers v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1984) 157 Cal.App.3d 914, 49 CCC 454

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (1st Dist.) (1984)
- **Category:** Domestic Services as Prescribed Medical Care
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4600

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Housekeeping, cleaning, and domestic services are reimbursable medical care under LC § 4600 when prescribed by a doctor to prevent medical complications, infection, or physical aggravation.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Housekeeping services are reimbursable as medical treatment under Labor Code section 4600 when they are medically necessary to cure or relieve the effects of the injury."
