# City of Petaluma v. WCAB (Lindh) (2018) 29 Cal.App.5th 1175, 83 CCC 1869

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (1st Dist.) (2018)
- **Category:** Asymptomatic Pre-existing Pathology Apportionment
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4663

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Apportionment to pre-existing, asymptomatic genetic or underlying disease pathology is legally permissible under LC § 4663 if the medical expert establishes that the pathology directly caused a percentage of disability.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Labor Code section 4663 requires apportionment to causation of disability, which includes pre-existing, non-industrial pathology that directly causes permanent disability even if asymptomatic prior to injury."
