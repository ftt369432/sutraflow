# Lundberg v. Workmen's Comp. App. Bd. (1968) 69 Cal.2d 436, 33 CCC 656

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1968)
- **Category:** AOE / COE & Liberal Construction
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3202, § 3600

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Under Labor Code § 3202, all reasonable doubts as to whether an injury arose out of and occurred in the course of employment must be resolved in favor of the injured worker.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Where the employee's work activities contribute to the disabling pathology, and there is doubt as to whether the injury is industrial, Labor Code section 3202 mandates that all doubts be resolved in favor of the employee."
