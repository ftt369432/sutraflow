# Myers v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1971) 20 Cal.App.3d 120, 36 CCC 568

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (1st Dist.) (1971)
- **Category:** Mandatory 7% Statutory Interest
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5800

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Interest at the rate of 7% per annum accrues automatically on all delayed indemnity and medical compensation from the date payment became due under LC § 5800.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Interest under Labor Code section 5800 is mandatory and runs from the date when each installment of compensation becomes due and payable."
