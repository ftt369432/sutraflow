# Castillo v. State of California (2014) 79 Cal.Comp.Cases 1090

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2014)
- **Category:** UR Notice & Communication Rules
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4610, 8 CCR § 9792.9.1(e)

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
UR decision must be communicated to the treating physician by telephone or fax within 24 hours of the decision and written notice served within 2 business days. Failure invalidates the UR.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Failure to communicate the UR decision to the treating physician within the mandatory regulatory timeframe renders the UR denial legally defective and of no force or effect."
