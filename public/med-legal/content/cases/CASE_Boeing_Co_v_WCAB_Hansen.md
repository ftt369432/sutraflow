# Boeing Co. v. WCAB (Hansen) (2015) 80 Cal.Comp.Cases 1338

- **Court:** Court of Appeal / WCAB Significant Panel (2015)
- **Category:** MPN Failure of Access Standards
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4616, 8 CCR § 9767.5

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
If an MPN fails to satisfy statutory geographic access standards or fails to provide an active, accurate physician directory upon request, the employee is legally entitled to treat outside the MPN.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Failure of the employer's MPN to meet access standards or provide an updated physician directory permits the employee to select a treating physician outside of the network."
