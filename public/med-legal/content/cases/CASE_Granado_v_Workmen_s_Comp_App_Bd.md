# Granado v. Workmen's Comp. App. Bd. (1968) 69 Cal.2d 399, 33 CCC 647

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1968)
- **Category:** No Apportionment of Temporary Disability or Medical Treatment
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4600, § 4650

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Temporary disability indemnity (TTD) and medical treatment (Labor Code § 4600) cannot be apportioned to non-industrial conditions. Even where permanent disability is apportioned, medical care and TTD are 100% the carrier's responsibility.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "There can be no apportionment of temporary disability or medical care; the employer is liable for the full cost of medical treatment and full temporary disability so long as employment is a contributing cause."
