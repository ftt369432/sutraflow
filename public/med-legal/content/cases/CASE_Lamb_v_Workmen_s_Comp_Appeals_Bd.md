# Lamb v. Workmen's Comp. Appeals Bd. (1974) 11 Cal.3d 274, 39 CCC 310

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1974)
- **Category:** AOE / COE & Vulnerability (Take Employee As You Find Him)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3600, § 3202

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
The employer takes the employee as he finds him (take the client/applicant as you find them). Industry takes the employee as it finds him. A pre-existing pathology or idiosyncratic predisposition does not preclude compensation where employment activities light up, aggravate, or accelerate the condition.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Industry takes the employee as it finds him. A person suffering from a pre-existing disease who is disabled by an abnormal strain or condition in his work is entitled to compensation even though a normal employee would not have been so disabled."
