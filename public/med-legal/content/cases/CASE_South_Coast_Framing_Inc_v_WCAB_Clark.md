# South Coast Framing, Inc. v. WCAB (Clark) (2015) 61 Cal.4th 291, 80 CCC 489

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (2015)
- **Category:** AOE / COE & Contributing Cause
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3600, § 4663

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Industrial causation requires only that employment be a contributing cause among others. Even a nominal industrial contribution (under 10%) triggers full joint & several liability.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "In workers' compensation law, an employee is entitled to compensation for an injury if the employment is a contributing cause of the injury... even if the industrial contribution is small."
