# Daniel Guerrero v. Ramcast Steel Fabrication (2017) 82 Cal.Comp.Cases 1222

- **Court:** WCAB Panel Decision (2017)
- **Category:** Violent Act (Mechanical Crushing Force)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4660.1(c)(2)(A)

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
A crushing accident caused by heavy industrial machinery constitutes a 'violent act' under LC § 4660.1(c)(2)(A), qualifying for the statutory exception allowing permanent psychiatric disability ratings.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An injury resulting from a mechanical crushing force by heavy machinery qualifies as a 'violent act' under Labor Code section 4660.1(c)(2)(A), permitting an award of permanent psychiatric disability."
