# Warren Brower v. David Jones Construction (2014) 79 Cal.Comp.Cases 550

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2014)
- **Category:** MPN Defense Inapplicable to Med-Legal
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4620, 8 CCR § 9793

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Employer MPNs have zero jurisdiction over medical-legal evaluations obtained by applicant's attorney to prove a contested claim under LC § 4060. MPN restrictions do not apply.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An employer or insurer has no authority under the MPN statutes to restrict an injured worker's attorney from selecting a medical-legal evaluator on a contested claim under Labor Code section 4060."
