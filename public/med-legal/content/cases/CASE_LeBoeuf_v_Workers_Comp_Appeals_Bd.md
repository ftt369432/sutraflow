# LeBoeuf v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1983) 34 Cal.3d 234, 48 CCC 587

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1983)
- **Category:** 100% PTD (Vocational Infeasibility)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4660, § 4662

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
An injured worker is 100% permanently and totally disabled (PTD) when industrial injuries prevent the worker from being educated, retrained, or benefiting from vocational rehabilitation.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The employee's inability to participate in vocational rehabilitation due to the industrial injury is a critical factor establishing 100% total permanent disability."
