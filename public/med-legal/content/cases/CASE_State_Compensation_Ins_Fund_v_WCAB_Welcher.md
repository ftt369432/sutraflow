# State Compensation Ins. Fund v. WCAB (Welcher) (1995) 31 Cal.App.4th 90, 60 CCC 27

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (3rd Dist.) (1995)
- **Category:** AOE / COE Presumptions (90-Day Rule)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5402(b)

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
If liability is not rejected within 90 days after the claim form is filed, the injury is presumed compensable; evidence discoverable during the 90 days with reasonable diligence is strictly barred.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The presumption of compensability is mandatory and requires the exclusion of all evidence which could have been obtained by the employer within the initial 90-day period with the exercise of reasonable diligence."
