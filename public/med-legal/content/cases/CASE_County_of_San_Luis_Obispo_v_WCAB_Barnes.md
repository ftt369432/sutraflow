# County of San Luis Obispo v. WCAB (Barnes) (2005) 133 Cal.App.4th 641, 70 CCC 1247

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (2nd Dist.) (2005)
- **Category:** Penalty Cap & Multipliers
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5814

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Under post-SB 899 LC § 5814, unreasonable delay penalties are 25% of the delayed amount up to a $10,000 maximum per separate, distinct act of unreasonable delay.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Section 5814 authorizes a penalty of up to 25 percent of the amount unreasonably delayed, not to exceed $10,000, for each separate and distinct act of delay."
