# Brinker Restaurant Corp. v. Superior Court (2012) 53 Cal.4th 1004

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (2012)
- **Category:** Employer Duty on Meal & Rest Periods
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 512, IWC Wage Orders

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
An employer satisfies its obligation to provide a 30-minute meal period by relieving the employee of all duty, relinquishing control over activities, and permitting them to leave the premises.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An employer's duty with respect to meal periods is to provide them by relieving the employee of all duty and relinquishing control over how the employee spends the time."
