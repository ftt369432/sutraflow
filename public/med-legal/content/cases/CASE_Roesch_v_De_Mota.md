# Roesch v. De Mota (1944) 24 Cal.2d 563

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1944)
- **Category:** Burden of Proof & Affirmative Issues
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5705, Evid. Code § 500

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
The party holding the affirmative of an issue bears the burden of proof. If that party fails to produce substantial evidence, the court has a mandatory duty to rule against them.   Waiver always rests upon intent. Waiver is the intentional relinquishment of a known right after knowledge of the facts. (Wienke v. Smith, 179 Cal. 220, 226 [176 P. 42]; 25 Cal.Jur. 926.) [3b]

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The burden of proof rests upon the party holding the affirmative of the issue... where the party with the burden fails to sustain it, the court must enter judgment against that party."
