# Braewood Convalescent Hospital v. WCAB (Bolton) (1983) 34 Cal.3d 159, 48 CCC 566

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1983)
- **Category:** Attendant Care & Medical Treatment Scope
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4600

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Medical treatment under Labor Code § 4600 broadly encompasses any treatment reasonably required to cure or relieve, including medically supervised weight reduction, attendant nursing care, and out-of-pocket lodging/travel expenses.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The employer must provide all treatment reasonably required to cure or relieve the employee from the effects of the injury, including attendant home care and specialized programs."
