# Contra Costa County v. WCAB (Dahl) (2015) 233 Cal.App.4th 223, 80 CCC 99

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (1st Dist.) (2015)
- **Category:** Post-SB 899 Vocational Rebuttal
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4660, § 4662

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Reaffirming LeBoeuf standard post-SB 899: An employee can rebut a scheduled PD rating and prove 100% PTD by showing an inability to compete in the labor market due to industrial physical limitations.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "A scheduled permanent disability rating under the 2005 PDRS may be rebutted by showing that the employee is not amenable to vocational rehabilitation under LeBoeuf."
