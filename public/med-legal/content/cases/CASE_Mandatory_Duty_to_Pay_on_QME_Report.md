# Mandatory Duty to Pay on QME Report Cal. Lab. Code § 4063

- **Court:** California Statute (1993)
- **Category:** Mandatory Duty to Pay upon QME Finding
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4063

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
When an agreed or qualified medical evaluator issues a formal evaluation resolving an issue requiring payment of benefits, the employer must immediately pay those benefits. Blanket refusal is a violation of law.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "If a formal medical evaluation from an agreed medical evaluator or a qualified medical evaluator resolves any issue so as to require an immediate payment of compensation or benefits, the employee shall be paid that compensation or benefits by the employer or the insurance carrier."
