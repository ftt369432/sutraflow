# Ballard v. Workmen's Comp. App. Bd. (1971) 3 Cal.3d 832, 36 CCC 34

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1971)
- **Category:** AOE / COE & Susceptibility (Eggshell Applicant)
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3600, § 4663

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
An employer takes the employee subject to his condition when he enters the employment; even if the employee is uniquely or unusually susceptible to injury or addiction from prescribed medications, full compensation is awarded without apportionment.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An employer takes the employee as he finds him at the time of employment. The employer may be held liable for disability resulting from the aggravation of a preexisting condition or idiosyncratic susceptibility."
