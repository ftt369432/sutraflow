# Wilson v. State of California, Cal Fire (2019) 84 Cal.Comp.Cases 393

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2019)
- **Category:** Catastrophic Injury Exception
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4660.1(c)(2)(B)

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Defines what constitutes a 'catastrophic injury' under LC § 4660.1(c)(2)(B). A catastrophic injury lifts the statutory bar on secondary psychiatric, sleep, and sexual dysfunction ratings, adding them directly to the PD rating.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Whether an injury is catastrophic under Labor Code section 4660.1(c)(2)(B) is evaluated based on the intensity and seriousness of treatment, ultimate outcome, severity of physical impairment, and impact on activities of daily living."
