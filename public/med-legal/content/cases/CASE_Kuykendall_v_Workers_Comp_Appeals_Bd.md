# Kuykendall v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (2000) 79 Cal.App.4th 396, 65 CCC 264

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (2nd Dist.) (2000)
- **Category:** Duty to Develop the Medical Record
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5701, § 5906

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
The WCAB and WCJ have an affirmative statutory duty to fully develop the medical record when the existing record is inadequate or incomplete, rather than rendering a 'take nothing' decision against the injured worker.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The Board has wide discretion and an affirmative duty to develop the record where the medical evidence is insufficient, ambiguous, or lacks foundation, to achieve substantial justice."
