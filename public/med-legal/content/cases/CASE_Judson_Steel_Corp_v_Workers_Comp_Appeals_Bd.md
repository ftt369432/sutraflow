# Judson Steel Corp. v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1978) 22 Cal.3d 658, 43 CCC 1205

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1978)
- **Category:** 132a Discrimination Shield
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 132a

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Labor Code § 132a prohibits an employer from discharging, threatening to discharge, or discriminating against an employee in any manner because the employee filed or declared an intent to file a workers' compensation claim.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Section 132a serves as a broad shield protecting employees from retaliation for exercising their rights under the workers' compensation laws."
