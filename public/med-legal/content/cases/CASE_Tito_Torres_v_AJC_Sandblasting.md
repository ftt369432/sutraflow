# Tito Torres v. AJC Sandblasting (2012) 77 Cal.Comp.Cases 1113

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2012)
- **Category:** Frivolous Litigation & Lien Proof Standard
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3202.5, § 5705, § 5813

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Proceeding to trial without evidence or with evidence utterly incapable of meeting the burden of proof is frivolous and bad faith under LC § 5813, justifying monetary sanctions and attorney's fees.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Proceeding to trial without any evidence or with evidence that is utterly incapable of meeting its burden of proof is frivolous and constitutes bad faith within the meaning of section 5813 justifying an award of sanctions, attorney's fees and costs."
