# Valdez v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (2013) 57 Cal.4th 1231, 78 CCC 1209

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (2013)
- **Category:** Admissibility of Non-MPN Medical Reports
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4600, § 4605, § 4616

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Medical reports from non-MPN treating physicians obtained under LC § 4605 are admissible in WCAB proceedings and can constitute substantial medical evidence.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Reports prepared by non-MPN treating physicians are admissible in proceedings before the WCAB... the Legislature did not intend to make non-MPN medical reports inadmissible."
