# Patterson v. The Oaks Farm (2014) 79 Cal.Comp.Cases 910

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2014)
- **Category:** Unilateral Termination of Ongoing Care Barred
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4610, § 4600

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
A defendant cannot unilaterally submit ongoing, previously authorized medical treatment (e.g. home care, medication) to UR to terminate it without demonstrating a change in medical circumstances.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "An employer cannot unilaterally terminate medical treatment that has been previously authorized and provided without showing a change in the employee's medical condition."
