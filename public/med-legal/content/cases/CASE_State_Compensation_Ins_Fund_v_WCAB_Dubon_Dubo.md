# State Compensation Ins. Fund v. WCAB (Dubon) (Dubon II) (2014) 79 Cal.Comp.Cases 1298

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2014)
- **Category:** Utilization Review (UR) & IMR Jurisdiction
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4610, § 4610.5, 8 CCR § 9792.9.1

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
If a Utilization Review (UR) decision is untimely or procedurally defective, the UR is void, IMR does not apply, and the WCAB has exclusive jurisdiction to decide medical necessity based on substantial evidence.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "When a utilization review decision is untimely and therefore invalid, the dispute over medical necessity is not subject to IMR, and the WCAB has jurisdiction to determine the medical necessity of the requested treatment based on substantial medical evidence."
