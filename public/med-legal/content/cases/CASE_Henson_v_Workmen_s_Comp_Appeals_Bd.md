# Henson v. Workmen's Comp. Appeals Bd. (1972) 27 Cal.App.3d 452, 37 CCC 564

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (4th Dist.) (1972)
- **Category:** Spousal Home Health Care Liens
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4600

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
An injured worker's spouse who provides extraordinary nursing and attendant care that would otherwise require hired professional nursing or institutionalization is entitled to reasonable reimbursement under LC § 4600.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The employer is liable for the reasonable value of nursing care provided by the wife where the employer had notice of the need for such services... the employer cannot escape liability merely because the care was rendered by a family member."
