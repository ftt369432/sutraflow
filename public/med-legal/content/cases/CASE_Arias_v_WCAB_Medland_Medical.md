# Arias v. WCAB (Medland Medical) ADJ16902178 (WCAB Panel Decision)

- **Court:** WCAB Panel Decision (2024)
- **Category:** Sanctions for Fabricated Citations
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5813, 8 CCR § 10421

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Submitting a pleading or petition containing a fabricated or hallucinated legal citation is sanctionable under LC § 5813 to protect judicial integrity, even if committed without intent to deceive.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The inclusion of a false or non-existent legal citation in a petition presented to the Appeals Board constitutes bad-faith, frivolous litigation conduct warranting sanctions under Labor Code section 5813."
