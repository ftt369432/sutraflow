# DPR Construction et al. v. WCAB (McClanahan) (2025) 107 Cal.App.5th ___ [2025 Cal. App. LEXIS 378]

- **Court:** Court of Appeal (Third Appellate Dist.) (2025)
- **Category:** Discovery Cutoff & Evidence Exclusion
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5502(d)(3), 8 CCR § 10670

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Discovery strictly closes at the MSC under LC § 5502(d)(3). Admitting evidence not listed on the Pre-Trial Conference Statement exceeds WCAB jurisdiction and is strictly NOT SUBJECT TO HARMLESS ERROR ANALYSIS. Lack of prejudice cannot excuse failure to list evidence.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Disregard of the statutory procedural mechanisms for resolving workers’ compensation cases is inappropriate... Such disregard is not subject to harmless error analysis... The absence of prejudice cannot be the deciding factor."
