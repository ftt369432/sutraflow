# Hartman v. WCAB / Sciarotta v. WCAB Hartman / Sciarotta Settlement Doctrine

- **Court:** Court of Appeal / WCAB Practice Standard (1985)
- **Category:** Settlement Allocations & Zero-MSA Formula
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5001

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Establishes the formula for lifetime allocation of Compromise and Release settlement proceeds across the injured worker's remaining life expectancy to minimize or eliminate Social Security Disability / Medicare offset.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Settlement proceeds in a Compromise and Release may be allocated over the injured worker's statistical life expectancy to calculate monthly amortization for federal benefit offsets."
