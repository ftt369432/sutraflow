# Escobedo v. Marshalls (2005) 70 Cal.Comp.Cases 604

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2005)
- **Category:** Apportionment Substantial Evidence
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4663, § 4664

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
To constitute substantial medical evidence on apportionment, a doctor must explain the exact nature of the apportioned pathology, how and why it causes permanent disability, and state exact percentages.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "A medical report is not substantial evidence on apportionment unless it explains how and why a particular non-industrial condition causes permanent disability... bare percentages without reasoned medical explanation are legally invalid."
