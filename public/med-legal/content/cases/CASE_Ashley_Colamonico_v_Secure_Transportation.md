# Ashley Colamonico v. Secure Transportation (2019) 84 Cal.Comp.Cases 1059

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2019)
- **Category:** Medical-Legal Contested Claim Standard
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4620, § 4621

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
To establish liability for medical-legal expenses under LC § 4620: (1) a contested claim must exist at the time of evaluation; (2) expenses must be incurred to prove or disprove the contested claim; and (3) services must be reasonable and necessary.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Medical-legal expenses are reimbursable when incurred for the purpose of proving or disproving a contested claim, provided the services were reasonable and necessary at the time rendered."
