# Romero v. Costco Wholesale (2007) 72 Cal.Comp.Cases 824

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2007)
- **Category:** Representation Voids Unrepresented QME Panel
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4062.1, § 4062.2

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
When an unrepresented worker retains counsel after a QME panel is issued under LC § 4062.1 but before an evaluation occurs, the unrepresented panel is void and the parties must proceed under represented panel rules (LC § 4062.2).

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "When an unrepresented employee retains counsel prior to the medical evaluation, the unrepresented QME panel process is terminated and the parties must initiate the represented panel process under Labor Code section 4062.2."
