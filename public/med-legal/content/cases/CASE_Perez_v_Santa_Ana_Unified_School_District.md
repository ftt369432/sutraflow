# Perez v. Santa Ana Unified School District (2023) 88 Cal.Comp.Cases 1

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2023)
- **Category:** Remote Witness Testimony & Virtual Trials
- **Controlling Authority:** 8 CCR § 10817, Labor Code § 5700

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
The WCAB has broad discretion to permit electronic and remote video testimony of injured workers, witnesses, and physicians upon a showing of good cause under 8 CCR § 10817.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The Appeals Board has the authority and discretion to order that a trial or hearing proceed electronically via video conference upon a showing of good cause."
