# Bodam v. San Bernardino County / Dept. of Social Services (2014) 79 Cal.Comp.Cases 977

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2014)
- **Category:** Utilization Review Timeframe Strictness
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 4610(g)(1), 8 CCR § 9792.9.1

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
UR statutory time limits (5 business days / 14 calendar days from RFA) are mandatory and strictly construed. An untimely UR decision is legally void and cannot be cured.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The timeframes in Labor Code section 4610 are mandatory... an untimely utilization review decision is invalid and ineffective to deny the requested medical treatment."
