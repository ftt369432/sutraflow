# Ramirez v. Drive Financial Services (2008) 73 Cal.Comp.Cases 1324

- **Court:** WCAB Appeals Board En Banc (2008)
- **Category:** Mandatory Attorney Fees on Delayed Pay
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 5814.5

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
When an employee successfully enforces payment of unreasonably delayed compensation under LC § 5814 against an unreasonable employer, the WCAB must award reasonable attorney's fees under LC § 5814.5.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "Labor Code section 5814.5 mandates an award of reasonable attorney's fees incurred in enforcing the payment of delayed compensation against an unreasonable employer or insurer."
