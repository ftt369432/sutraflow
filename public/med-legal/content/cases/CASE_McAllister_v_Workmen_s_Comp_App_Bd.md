# McAllister v. Workmen's Comp. App. Bd. (1968) 69 Cal.2d 408, 33 CCC 660

- **Court:** California Supreme Court (1968)
- **Category:** AOE / COE & Causation
- **Controlling Authority:** Labor Code § 3600, § 3202.5

## 🏛️ Holding & Principle
Proof of industrial causation requires only 'reasonable medical probability' (more likely than not), not absolute scientific certainty or mathematical precision.

## 📜 Key Verbatim Quote for Pleadings
> "The applicant need only establish the reasonable probability of industrial causation. He is not required to prove causation with scientific certainty."
