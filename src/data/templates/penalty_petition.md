Labor Code § 5814 states: "When payment of compensation has been unreasonably delayed or refused, either prior to or subsequent to the issuance of an award, the full amount of the order, decision, or award shall be increased by 10 percent." 

In this case, Defendant’s delay of [[DAYS_DELAYED]] days is per se unreasonable as it violates CCR § 10109 and the mandate in *County of San Joaquin v. WCAB*.
