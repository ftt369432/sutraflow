INTRODUCTION

This trial brief is written to assist the court in its findings and to frame the issues and facts surrounding this case, in support of a finding of reasonableness and necessity of the medical services provided by Dr. Omid Haghighinia.

Under Colamonico v. Secure Transportation (2019) 84 Cal.Comp.Cases 1059 (Appeals Board en banc), a lien claimant must establish:
(1) a contested claim at the time of service;
(2) that the services were to prove or disprove that claim; and
(3) that the services were reasonable and necessary.

All three prongs are satisfied here.
