COMES NOW the applicant ADOLFO CASTRO, files this Penalty Petition Under Labor Code 5814, regarding temporary disability benefits that have been unreasonably refused or delayed, and seeks an Order of this Board assessing penalties against all such benefits not paid, under Lab. Code Sections §5814 and §5813 as applicable, and in support thereof allege that:

FACTS

1.	Applicant has filed an Application for Adjudication for date of Injury of April 12, 2023, occurring on April 12, 2023, occurring in the course of employment as a metal fabricator and employed by TTL Holdings, when the applicant and a co worker was lifting a large piece of sheet metal, and the co worker let go of the object allowing they weight to fall on the applicant shoulder, causing a tear in the left shoulder and strain of the neck.  Injury has been plead to the bilateral shoulders and bilateral hands.  
2.	On September 6, 2024, Applicant elected Dr. Mohammad Rockny Mojabe, D.C. from defendant's medical provider network, Corvel Corporation, at the web address provided.  
3.	On November 15, 2024, Defendant sent	 NOTICE REGARDING TEMPORARY BENEFITS PAYMENT TERMINATION, as they claim they have not received evidence for substantiation of ongoing benefits.  
4.	On December 13, 2024, a demand for Temporary Disability Benefits was sent to defendants; however, it does not appear that benefits commenced, and as such, the applicant was forced to self-procure benefits from E.D.D. 

ARGUMENT

1.	APPLICANT IS ENTITLED TO TEMPORARY DISABILITY BENEFITS AS CERTIFIED BY HIS PRIMARY TREATING PHYSICIAN, WHO IS PART OF THE DEFENDANTS MEDICAL PROVIDER NETWORK. APPLICANT REQUESTS THE COURT ORDER RETROACTIVE BENEFITS, INCLUDING A PENALTY UNDER LABOR CODE 5814:
Defendants are responsible for indemnity payments to Applicant based upon his PTP, Dr. Mohammad Rockny Mojabe, D.C, who is part of the defendant's Medical Provider Network.  A Notice of Change in physicians, as provided under Labor Code 4600 and 4601, was submitted to the defendants, who chose to stop the Applicant's TTD benefits despite being duly notified.  The defendants have denied the benefits, as well as reasonable medical care to the Applicant; he was forced to obtain benefits from the stater E.D.D. office.  
On December 13, 2024, the Applicant's counsel sent a demand for TTD benefits, based upon the PTP’s findings, to be paid retrospectively.  At this time, it does not appear that a response has been received.  Applicant has now exhausted EDD as the defendants failed to commence benefits after being duly notified. Applicant now seeks relief from the court.  

 Labor Code  § 5814 provides that when payment of compensation has been “unreasonably delayed or refused, either prior to or subsequent to the issuance of an award,” the full amount of the delayed or refused compensation may be increased by 25 percent.
The medical findings from the Applicant's treating physicians, Dr. Mohammad Rockny Mojabe, D.C., supports the Applicant's entitlement to retrospective temporary disability benefits.  Defendants were obligated to initiate indemnity payments promptly, as mandated by Labor Code § 4650. Despite being duly notified, as well as the submission of a formal demand on December 13, 2024, defendants have unreasonably delayed the commencement of benefits, violating both statutory and regulatory requirements, including breaching their duties under California Code of Regulations § 10109(c).
As such, under Labor Code § 5814, the Applicant respectfully requests a 25% increase in compensation for all benefits wrongfully delayed.  

2.	APPLICANT SEEKS AN AWARD OF LABOR CODE 5814 PENALTIES OF 25% FOR THE PERIOD OF TIME EDD WAS ADMINISTERING BENEFITS, INCLUDING AN ORDER FOR DEFENDANTS TO REIMBURSE EDD IN FULL:
Due to the defendant's denial of Temporary Disability Benefits, as certified by his MPN provider, the Applicant was forced to obtain benefits from EDD, which are scheduled to be terminated.  Further, rather than authorize reasonable and necessary care from an MPN provider, the defendants unreasonably denied medical care to the Applicant.
Labor Code  § 5814 provides that when payment of compensation has been “unreasonably delayed or refused, either prior to or subsequent to the issuance of an award,” the full amount of the delayed or refused compensation may be increased by 25 percent.

Therefore, Petitioner requests the Court order the reimbursement of EDD, and the applicant’s claimed penalty of 25% on all TTD monies unreasonably delayed or withheld.  

3.	DEFENDANTS' UNREASONABLE DELAY IN PROVIDING MEDICAL TREATMENT, INCLUDING MEDICAL MILEAGE, WARRANTS PENALTIES FOR ALL FUTURE MEDICAL CARE UNDER LABOR CODE 5814:
Defendants’ failure to authorize and provide the necessary medical treatment for the Applicant’s industrial injuries constitutes an unreasonable delay, as defined by Labor Code § 5814. Under this provision, if compensation, including medical treatment, is unreasonably delayed or refused, the payment can be increased by up to 25%, or up to $10,000, whichever is less. 
California Labor Code § 4600 mandates that an employer must provide medical treatment that is "reasonably required to cure or relieve the effects of the injury." 
The Applicant respectfully requests that the court impose a 25% penalty on all delayed or refused medical benefits to ensure compliance and uphold the principles of California’s workers’ compensation system.

4.	DEFENDANT IS LIABLE FOR 10% PER ANNUM INTEREST ON ANY MONEY NOT PAID.  
Pursuant to Labor Code § 5800, when compensation payments, are unreasonably delayed, the injured worker is entitled to receive interest on the delayed amount.  The purpose of this statute is to ensure that injured workers are compensated for the loss of use of funds that were rightfully due to them and to promote timely compliance with payment obligations by employers and insurers.
Labor Code § 5800 mandates that all awards from the appeals board for compensation or death benefits are subject to interest, calculated at 10% per annum on all delayed payments of compensation, including Temporary disability benefits, Permanent disability benefits, Medical treatment costs, Mileage reimbursement, Vocational rehabilitation costs, and any other form of compensation that has been delayed.  
Therefore, the Applicant requests an award of 10% interest, to be applied to all benefits the Applicant is entitled to.  

5.	APPLICANT SEEKS AN ORDER FOR ATTORNEY FEES UNDER LABOR CODE 5814.5 AND LABOR CODE 5813 FOR DEFENDANTS' UNREASONABLE DELAY OF BENEFITS:
In this case, the defendants have accepted the specific injury for the applicant's left shoulder, as the applicant underwent surgery.  At this time, however, Defendants have denied the Applicant TTD benefits.  As there was no response to the Applicant’s demand for benefits, the Applicant was forced to obtain relief from E.D.D.  Applicant’s Attorney has had to file this Petition for Penalties on all benefits that have been unreasonably delayed. 
Labor Code § 5813 allows the Workers’ Compensation Appeals Board to award reasonable expenses, including attorney fees and costs, when a party engages in bad-faith actions or tactics that are frivolous or solely intended to cause unnecessary delay. 
Applicant’s attorney has had to prepare this petition, file a Declaration of Readiness (DOR), and will be required to appear at court to resolve these issues, incurring substantial time and effort. Attorney fees are sought, time at $500.00 per hour is pled as follows:
