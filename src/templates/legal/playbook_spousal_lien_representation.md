# 🏛️ STRATEGIC PLAYBOOK: REPRESENTING THE SPOUSE IN AN EAMS HOME HEALTHCARE LIEN

**Subject:** Procedural, Ethical, and Structural Roadmap for representing the **{{spouse_name}}** as Lien Claimant while representing the **{{applicant_name}}** as Applicant.  
**Legal Framework:** California Rules of Professional Conduct (CRPC) Rule 1.7, Labor Code § 4903(b), § 4903.05, § 4903.2, and § 4906.

---

## ⚖️ 1. ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS & CONFLICT WAIVERS (CRPC RULE 1.7)

Representing both the injured worker (Applicant) and their spouse (Lien Claimant) is a common and powerful strategy in high-value workers' compensation cases. However, under **California Rules of Professional Conduct (CRPC) Rule 1.7 (Conflict of Interest Between Current Clients)**, you must secure **Informed Written Consent** from both parties to prevent any ethical challenges by the defense.

### **Why their interests are currently aligned:**
*   Both parties seek to prove that the home healthcare services were medically necessary and that the insurance carrier failed to provide them.
*   Any recovery for the spouse’s lien directly increases the financial recovery for the family unit.

### **Where a potential conflict could arise:**
*   If the carrier offers a lump-sum global settlement (e.g., C&R) that includes a fixed pot of money to resolve both the applicant's future medical care and the spousal lien, and the parties must negotiate how to divide that pot.

### **Action Item: Execute a Conflict Waiver**
Before filing the lien representing the spouse, have both the Applicant and the Spouse sign the following waiver:

> ### **INFORMED WRITTEN CONSENT & CONFLICT OF INTEREST WAIVER**
> *Under California Rules of Professional Conduct, Rule 1.7*
>

The Firm has also been requested to represent **{{spouse_name}}** ("Lien Claimant / Spouse") in filing and litigating her spousal home healthcare/attendant care lien in the amount of **${{total_billed_amount}}**.
>

## 💰 2. ATTORNEY FEES ON LIEN RECOVERY (LABOR CODE § 4903.2 & § 4906)

Representing the spouse on the lien allows the firm to recover an **additional attorney fee** directly out of the lien recovery, separate from the standard 15% applicant attorney fee.

*   **Labor Code § 4903.2 (The Fee-Shifting Provision):** If the attorney representing the applicant also represents the lien claimant, and successfully prosecutes a denied medical treatment lien, the WCJ may award a **reasonable attorney’s fee** to be paid out of the lien recovery.
*   **The Standard Fee:** The standard fee for representing a lien claimant is **12% to 15% of the recovered amount**, depending on the complexity of the litigation and whether a deposition or trial was required.
*   **For the Spouse's Lien:** A 15% fee on a full recovery of **${{total_billed_amount}}** represents an additional **${{attorney_fee_amount}}** in attorney fees for the firm, approved by the WCJ and paid out of the spousal recovery.

## ⚙️ 3. EAMS FILING MECHANICS (STEP-BY-STEP)

When filing the standard lien representing the spouse in EAMS, the filing clerk must execute the following steps precisely:

### **Step 1: Set Up Spouse as a Lien Claimant in EAMS**
*   If the spouse does not have an active EAMS Lien Claimant ID, they must be registered.
*   Associate the **{{law_firm_name}}** (using the firm’s EAMS Uniform Name / U-Name) as their designated **Lien Representative**.

### **Step 2: Complete DWC Form 6 (Lien Request)**
*   **Type of Lien:** Select **Medical Treatment Expense (Labor Code § 4903(b))**. *(Spousal attendant care is legally categorized as self-procured medical treatment under Henson).*
*   **Lien Amount:** **${{total_billed_amount}}**.
*   **Service Dates:** {{phase1_start_date}} to {{phase4_end_date}}.
*   **Provider of Services:** {{spouse_name}}.

### **Step 3: Pay the $150.00 Mandatory Filing Fee (If Applicable)**
*   Under **Labor Code § 4903.05**, the $150.00 filing fee must be paid **electronically at the time of filing** via the EAMS credit card/debit card gateway. 
*   *Note:* The firm can advance this cost as a case expense, to be reimbursed upon recovery. If the fee is not paid instantly, EAMS will reject and invalidate the filing. 
*   *Free Option Workaround:* The DWC Form 6 can also be submitted under the Applicant's own case-in-chief seeking reimbursement for self-procured medical treatment. When filed directly under the Applicant's case-in-chief, no filing fee is required.

### **Step 4: Upload the Supporting Evidence Packet**
You must upload the following integrated files as a single "Lien Evidence Packet" in EAMS:
1.  **Notice of Lien Cover Sheet & Proof of Service.**
2.  **Declaration of Spouse** (signed under penalty of perjury).
3.  **Exhibit A: Itemized Bill of Services** (the Step-Down Table).
4.  **Exhibit B: Medical Support** (Excerpts of treating physician reports detailing ADL deficits and discharge orders).

### **Step 5: Serve the Parties (8 CCR § 10850)**
Serve the completed DWC Form 6 and the entire Evidence Packet via first-class mail or secure fax to:
*   **Defense Counsel:** {{defendant_attorney_name}} of {{defendant_law_firm_name}}.
*   **Claims Administrator:** {{defendant_insurance_carrier_name}}.
*   **Applicant:** {{applicant_name}}.

## 🏁 4. LITIGATION ADVANTAGES OF THIS PATHWAY

1.  **Designated Party Status:** As an official Lien Claimant represented by counsel, the spouse is a **party in interest** with standing to appear at hearings, file petitions, and participate directly in conferences. The defense cannot ignore them.
2.  **Double Leverage at Trial:** At the trial, the firm will appear both as **Attorney for Applicant** and **Attorney for Lien Claimant (Spouse)**. This prevents the defense from trying to split the claims or settle the main case while leaving the spousal care exposed.
3.  **Firm Profitability:** Securing the dual representation protects the family's assets while locking in an additional WCJ-approved fee of up to 15% for the firm’s litigation work.
