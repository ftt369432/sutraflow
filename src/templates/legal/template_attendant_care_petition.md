### **IV. LEGAL ARGUMENT & PRECEDENT**

10. **Statutory Authority:** Under Labor Code § 4600, the employer is liable for all medical treatment reasonably required to cure or relieve the effects of the injury, which explicitly includes home nursing and attendant care services.
11. **The Landmark Henson Holding:** In *Henson v. WCAB* (1972) 27 Cal.App.3d 452, the Court of Appeal established that spousal caregiver services are fully reimbursable if: (1) the employer had knowledge of the medical need for such care, and (2) the employer failed to provide it. The *Henson* court explicitly held that when a spouse performs skilled or semi-skilled nursing duties that go beyond normal household chores, they are entitled to reasonable compensation at the market rate.
12. **Satisfaction of Henson Prongs:** Here, both prongs of *Henson* are satisfied. Defendant had continuous notice of Applicant's catastrophic deconditioning and medical care needs, yet completely failed to act. Consequently, {{spouse_name}}'s self-procured care is fully reimbursable.
13. **Lien Attorney Fees:** Because Applicant's counsel represents both {{applicant_name}} and {{spouse_name}}, and is successfully litigating this denied medical treatment benefit, Applicant further requests that the WCJ award a reasonable attorney's fee to be paid out of the recovered lien amount pursuant to Labor Code § 4903.2.
